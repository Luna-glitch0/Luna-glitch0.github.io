에셋

JAEN 스토리에 실제로 쓴 레퍼런스 14장입니다. 이미지를 누르면 원본 크기로 열립니다.

레퍼런스는 «무엇을 정의하는가» 를 문장으로 못 박아야 쓸모가 있습니다.

역할을 지정하지 않은 레퍼런스는 서로 다툽니다. 캐릭터 시트는 분할 화면과 스튜디오 배경을 가지고 있어서, 그대로 넣으면 결과에 회색 배경이나 인물 둘이 딸려 나옵니다. 그래서 각 묶음 아래에 그 에셋과 함께 붙이는 지시문을 두었습니다.

Part 1

시작 프레임

구도를 먼저 확정합니다. 왜곡이 가장 적었던 방식입니다. 시작 프레임을 고르는 것이 곧 씬을 고르는 것입니다.

프레임과 지시가 어긋나면 둘 중 하나가 버려집니다.

«문 열림» 프레임에 이동 지시를 붙이거나 «안내» 프레임에 고정 카메라 지시를 붙이면 모델은 둘 중 하나를 버립니다. 어느 쪽을 버릴지는 고를 수 없습니다.

함께 붙이는 지시문

@Image 1 IS THE FIRST FRAME. The shot begins exactly on it - same framing,
same objects in the same places, same light. Nothing is re-staged or added at the start.

Continue directly from the supplied starting frame.

Match the starting frame's camera position, framing, Hinata's facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, door opening angle, hand placement, lighting, and entrance layout.
Part 2

인물

얼굴은 정면 포트레이트가, 옷과 비율은 전신이 정합니다. 역할을 나눠 주는 것이 핵심입니다.

함께 붙이는 지시문

[ 역할 분리 ]
All three images depict ONE AND THE SAME woman. Show only one Hinata.
Keep each reference assigned to its specified subject.
Do not reproduce studio backgrounds, reference-sheet layouts, duplicate portraits, or split screens.
Hinata's references apply ONLY to the woman inside the doorway.
Do not transfer her pink sweater to the first-person visitor.

[ 얼굴과 몸을 나눈다 ]
Use the detailed facial portrait for facial identity and the full-body view for clothing and proportions.

[ 표정 ]
Do not hold the reference expression as a fixed pose or exaggerate the size of her eyes.
Preserve her normal facial proportions.
No exaggerated wide eyes, stretched mouth, or theatrical shock.

[ 방문자 의상 - 소매만 ]
Use ONLY for the unseen first-person visitor's white cotton sweatshirt sleeves and ribbed cuffs.
Do not generate this reference person standing in the scene.
Do not copy the reference person's face, hairstyle, or full-body pose into the video.
Part 3

배경

문턱 · 바닥 단차 · 문 · 계단 · 가구가 씬마다 같아야 합니다.

함께 붙이는 지시문

[ 실내 ]
Do not mirror or rearrange the layout.
This image defines the interior space, not a separate shot.

[ 실외 - 가로 구도를 따라가지 않게 ]
Use the architecture as reference, not the photograph's wide composition.
Recompose the entrance naturally for vertical 9:16; do not reproduce the wide framing or squeeze the entire house into view.

[ 프레임 밖으로 나가도 된다 ]
Frame only what is naturally visible from the visitor's position.
Allow the roof, ground, suitcase, and Hinata's lower body to extend outside the frame.
Do not widen the lens to fit everything into one image.
Part 4

소품

«어디에 남는지» 까지 정해야 소품이 카메라를 따라오지 않습니다.

함께 붙이는 지시문

[ 일부만 프레임에 ]
Only the handle and a small portion of the case need to enter the lower-left edge.
Do not force the whole suitcase into view.

[ 놓은 뒤에는 남는다 ]
The visitor parks the suitcase upright beside their left leg, clear of the front door's swing and the path onto the wooden floor, and releases the handle.
발췌를 그대로 복사하지 않습니다.

«세 장» 은 그때의 레퍼런스 개수를, «현관» 은 그 씬을 전제합니다. 실제 개수와 씬에 맞게 고쳐 씁니다.

Assets

The fourteen references actually used on the JAEN story. Click an image to open it at full size.

A reference is only useful once a sentence pins down what it defines.

References without assigned roles fight each other. Character sheets carry split-screen layouts and studio backdrops, so dropped in as-is they bring a grey background or a second copy of the person along with them. That is why each group below ends with the instructions that ship with those assets.

Part 1

Start frames

Establish the composition first — this produced the least distortion. Choosing the start frame is choosing the scene.

A frame that contradicts the direction loses one of the two.

Attach relocation directions to the “door open” frame, or stationary-camera directions to the “leading in” frame, and the model drops one of them. You do not get to choose which.

Instructions that ship with a start frame

@Image 1 IS THE FIRST FRAME. The shot begins exactly on it - same framing,
same objects in the same places, same light. Nothing is re-staged or added at the start.

Continue directly from the supplied starting frame.

Match the starting frame's camera position, framing, Hinata's facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, door opening angle, hand placement, lighting, and entrance layout.

Cast

The portrait defines the face; the full body defines clothing and proportions. Splitting the roles is the point.

Instructions that ship with cast references

[ Role separation ]

Keep each reference assigned to its specified subject.
Do not reproduce studio backgrounds, reference-sheet layouts, duplicate portraits, or split screens.
Hinata's references apply ONLY to the woman inside the doorway.
Do not transfer her pink sweater to the first-person visitor.

[ Expression ]

Preserve her normal facial proportions.

Part 3

Locations

Thresholds, floor levels, doors, stairs, and furniture must match across scenes.

Instructions that ship with location references

[ Interior ]
Do not mirror or rearrange the layout.
This image defines the interior space, not a separate shot.

[ Exterior - do not inherit the landscape composition ]
Use the architecture as reference, not the photograph's wide composition.
Recompose the entrance naturally for vertical 9:16; do not reproduce the wide framing or squeeze the entire house into view.

[ Things are allowed to leave the frame ]
Frame only what is naturally visible from the visitor's position.
Allow the roof, ground, suitcase, and Hinata's lower body to extend outside the frame.
Do not widen the lens to fit everything into one image.
Part 4

Props

Decide where a prop stays, or it follows the camera.

Instructions that ship with the prop

[ Only part of it in frame ]
Only the handle and a small portion of the case need to enter the lower-left edge.
Do not force the whole suitcase into view.

[ It stays where it was put ]
The visitor parks the suitcase upright beside their left leg, clear of the front door's swing and the path onto the wooden floor, and releases the handle.
Do not copy the excerpts verbatim.

“All three images” assumes that reference count and “the doorway” assumes that scene. Update them to match the actual count and scene.
