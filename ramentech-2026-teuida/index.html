日会話シミュレーション型の語学学習
実生活を再現した一人称視点のシミュレーションを通じて、韓国語などを実際に話しながら学べる語学学習アプリです。AIが発音を評価し、短時間でも継続的に会話練習ができる、没入型の学習体験を提供します。

ENSpeaking-first language learning through interactive real-life simulations
Teuida is a speaking-first language learning application that places learners inside interactive, first-person scenarios based on real-life conversations. Users practice speaking from the beginning while AI evaluates pronunciation and responses. Visitors can experience its immersive approach to learning Korean and other languages without travelling abroad.
